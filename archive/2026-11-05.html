Our model's picks: win chance > 50%, odds ≥ 1.50, positive value. Value = model win % × Sky/selection odds − 1 (shown for information; historically also required for eligibility).
How the score works. Every team starts at 100 points. 1 point is a 1% change in that team's goal-scoring edge (100 × the model's log goal-rate terms). Attack and defence come from a time-weighted Dixon-Coles model of results. Home advantage is fitted for each league. Form is the last 6 results against what the model expected (capped at ±5, not backtested). Absences are a judgement value split across the named players. Home score minus away score gives the expected-goals ratio, then the Poisson scoreline grid is blended with Elo to give win %. Value = model % × selection odds − 1. Tiers (informational): A = value ≥ 6% and model ≥ 55%; B = value ≥ 3%; C = otherwise. The model always produces a full home/draw/away probability (Dixon–Coles Poisson blended with Elo). The predicted side is whichever of those three is highest; official picks still need that probability > 50% and odds ≥ the minimum. Draws rarely clear 50% alone.
Honest caveat: in our walk-forward backtest the bookies' odds were more accurate (log loss 0.954 vs 0.973). These are model-first picks, not a proven edge. Odds move, and best prices come from different bookmakers.
